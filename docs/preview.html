🌱 Beginner guide — zero to interview-ready

Step 0. The one idea. Models learn by predicting the next word until good text runs out. Kimi k1.5 lets models practice problems and learn from right/wrong — reinforcement learning.
Step 1. Overthinking. First you stumble into the answer (+1). Then a friend asks how, and you talk for five minutes. One good sentence hides inside. Models do the same — which is why we compress.
Step 2. Work it out step-by-step. "Let's work this out in a step-by-step way to be sure we have the right answer." Say it, then do it: (a) restate the question, (b) list facts, (c) compute twice, (d) box the answer. Long thinking made visible — then we cut the filler.
Step 3. The four compressors. Merge fast+deep thinkers · keep shortest-correct · train short-beats-long preference · penalize length in a second training round.
Step 4. Why pictures & tests. Charts ground words in the world (future agents). Generated tests give clean pass/fail where web tests are flaky.
Step 5. Interview line. "RL scales past data limits but overthinks; Long2Short recovers efficiency — ~36% free from shortest-correct, tuned penalties best, budgets by difficulty, verified on live data."
